**Effective date: August 31, 2026**

Presentation Click is a Bluetooth remote control app that lets you control a computer presentation from an Android device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information the app uses

Presentation Click uses Bluetooth permissions to discover, pair with, and communicate with the computer you choose to control. Bluetooth device names and connection status may be displayed in the app, but they are used only to provide the remote-control function.

The app stores your selected language, display mode, volume-key preference, and other app settings locally on your device. These settings are not sent to us.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it only to respond to you.

## Advertising and purchases

The app may display advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process information under their own privacy policies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising. You can review Google's policies at [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/).

The app may also use Google Play Billing to process an optional purchase to remove advertisements. Payment details are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your full payment card information.

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate an account system for Presentation Click. Information handled by third-party advertising and payment services is subject to those services' policies. Email correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain support records.

## Children's privacy

Presentation Click is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
